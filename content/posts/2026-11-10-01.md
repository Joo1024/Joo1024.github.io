## 评估投资策略的四种方法

https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/backtesting-and-simulation

下面的英文摘自 CFA Institute 的《Backtesting & Simulation》，括号里也记了我对几个概念的直觉。

- Four techniques used to evaluate investment strategies. 
  - **回测 / backtesting**, tests a strategy in a historical environment, usually over long periods, answering the question “How would this strategy have performed if it were implemented in the past?” 
    - 滚动窗口回测 / rolling-window backtesting: The basic steps in rolling-window backtesting are specifying the investment hypothesis and goals, determining the rules and processes behind an investment strategy, forming an investment portfolio according to those rules, rebalancing the portfolio periodically, and computing the performance and risk profiles of the strategy. In the rolling-window backtesting methodology, researchers use a rolling-window (or walk-forward) framework, fit/calibrate factors or trade signals based on the rolling window, rebalance the portfolio periodically, and then track the performance over time. Asset (and factor) returns are often negatively skewed and exhibit excess kurtosis (fat tails) and tail dependence compared with a normal distribution. As a result, standard rolling-window backtesting may be unable to fully account for the randomness in asset returns, particularly on downside risk. 
  - **历史情景分析 / historical scenario analysis / historical stress testing**, examines the efficacy of a strategy in discrete historical environments, such as during recessions or periods of high inflation. Financial data often face structural breaks. Scenario analysis can help investors understand the performance of an investment strategy in different structural regimes.
  - **模拟 / simulation**, explores how a strategy would perform in a hypothetical environment specified by the user, rather than a historical setting; it is a useful complement to other methods because the past may not recur and only a limited number of all possible future observations for important variables (e.g., interest rates, return correlations, economic growth) is represented in history. 
    - 蒙特卡洛模拟 / Monte Carlo simulation: Monte Carlo simulation is a more sophisticated technique than historical simulation. In Monte Carlo simulation, the most important decision is the choice of functional form of the statistical distribution of decision variables/ return drivers. Multivariate normal distribution (多元正态分布) is often used in investment research, owing to its simplicity. However, a multivariate normal distribution cannot account for negative skewness (负偏, 对应的现实直觉: 平时小赚, 但偶尔一次巨亏) and fat tails (厚尾, 对应的现实直觉: 极端涨跌比正态模型估计得更常见) observed in factor and asset returns.
    - 历史模拟 / historical simulation: Historical simulation is relatively straightforward to perform but shares pros and cons similar to those of rolling-window backtesting. For example, a key assumption these methods share is that the distribution pattern from the historical data is sufficient to represent the uncertainty in the future. Bootstrapping (or random draws with replacement) is often used in historical simulation.
  - **敏感性分析 / sensitivity analysis**, which is often combined with simulation to uncover the impact of changing key assumptions. Sensitivity analysis, a technique for exploring how a target variable and risk profiles are affected by changes in input variables, can further help investors understand the limitations of conventional Monte Carlo simulation (which typically assumes a multivariate normal distribution as a starting point). A multivariate skewed t-distribution considers skewness and kurtosis but requires estimation of more parameters and thus is more likely to suffer from larger estimation errors (偏斜t分布能同时描述“左右不对称”和“极端值更常见”, 但尾部参数更难估准 ; 正态模型简单, 但可能漏掉尾部风险 ; 尾部风险估计很依赖分布假设).
- Analysts need to pay attention to several behavioral issues in backtesting, including survivorship bias and look-ahead bias.

## Flowfolio 如何承接这四种方法

在学习完上述检验投资策略的四种方法后, 我发现, `Flowfolio` 现有的框架可以作为它们共用的执行底座。`Scenario` 接收一条符合协议的行情路径, 以及初始账户、入金和费用等条件。无论行情来自真实历史还是模拟生成, 都可以用同一个 `run()` 执行策略, 再用 `evaluate()` 按统一口径计算收益、回撤和费用。做滚动窗口回测时, 我按时间顺序使用真实历史数据, 每次只用当时可见的信息形成决策, 再跟踪后续表现。做历史情景分析时, 我选取金融危机或高通胀等特定历史区间, 比较策略和基准在同一段行情中的表现。做历史模拟或蒙特卡洛模拟时, 我分别从历史数据中抽样或从概率模型中生成多条行情路径, 把每条路径作为一个 `Scenario` 运行, 再汇总结果分布。做敏感性分析时, 我固定其他条件, 有计划地改变某个输入。这个输入可以是 `Scenario` 里的费用或入金, 也可以是策略的回撤阈值, 或生成行情所用的分布假设。这样, 研究层负责选取或生成路径、安排对照和汇总结果, `Flowfolio` 负责让每次运行都能按相同规则执行和核对。

如果策略需要定期校准参数，滚动窗口就要分清两段数据。比如每月用此前 12 个月的数据校准参数，再用这组参数跑接下来一个月。窗口向前移一个月后，再校准、再检验，最后把各月的样本外表现按时间接起来。

并且, `Scenario → Run → RunResult` 这条主线可以定下来。`Scenario` 提供初始账户、行情、入金和执行假设, `Strategy` 根据只读快照返回动作, `Run` 绑定场景、策略及其生效参数、执行器, 并持有单次运行状态。Reference 和 Backtrader 通过同一协议推进时序、报告成交并提交估值。这样既能固定场景换策略, 也能固定策略换场景, 或者固定两者换执行器核对流水。

## portfolio 研究中的待解问题

还梳理了一下在portfolio研究中尚存的问题:

1. VXUS 在组合里要解决什么问题？
2. VO 和 VB 分别提供了什么规模暴露？同时持有两只时, 相比现有宽基组合, 实际多覆盖了哪些公司和风险？
3. 确定目标权重后, 按目标比例拆单、优先补金额缺口最大的资产、每次集中买一只, 这些规则在配置偏离、交易笔数和总费用上各有什么差异？
4. 考虑单价、碎股可用性和每笔手续费, 入金多大时值得拆单？金额较小时, 立即买入和累积几笔再买的差别有多大？
5. 美元计价的美元货基和港元计价的美元货基, 按同一持有期和最终用钱币种比较, 扣掉换汇、划转和赎回成本后, 各剩多少净收益？
6. 对比境内 QDII 和境外 ETF 时, 把产品费用、换汇价差、划转成本、申购额度和资金在途时间都算进去, 实际差距有多大？

其中, 第三个问题关于三种入金分配规则, 可以用 flowfolio 框架直接检验, 同时也能丰富 flowfolio 的功能. 固定目标权重、初始持仓、行情、入金和费用，分别跑三条规则。费用可直接评价，订单笔数从流水统计，配置偏离可用逐期持仓和收盘价计算。这里还需要写清「每次集中买一只」怎么选标的。若也是选金额缺口最大的那只，两条规则的区别可以落在余款怎么处理。比如补足最大缺口后还有剩余，一条规则继续补下一只，另一条当天只买这一只，余款留在现金里。

另外, 根据我自己的研究兴趣, 前两个问题最先开始.
